Porsche

1.Introduction

Porsche is a famous German automobile manufacturer known for producing luxury sports cars, SUVs, and electric vehicles.
- Company name: Dr. Ing. h.c. F. Porsche AG
- Founded: 1931
- Founder: Ferdinand Porsche
- Headquarters: Stuttgart, Germany
- Industry: Automobile manufacturing
- Famous for: Luxury, speed, performance, and stylish design
- Popular model: Porsche 911

2. History of Porsche

Porsche was founded by Ferdinand Porsche in 1931.
Initially, the company worked on automobile engineering and vehicle development.
In 1948, Porsche introduced its first car, the Porsche 356.
In 1964, the iconic Porsche 911 was introduced.
Today, Porsche manufactures sports cars, SUVs, and electric cars.
